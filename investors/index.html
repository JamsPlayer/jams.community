Investors & grant makers

Music without a middleman holding the money.

Jams is a music ecosystem where listeners and artists deal directly. The apps run on people’s own devices, the shared data lives on an open storage network, and the company never takes custody of anyone’s funds, data or work.

The problem

Independent artists sit at the end of a long payment chain. Platforms, distributors and pools each take a cut and set the terms, and the listener has no way to know how much of what they pay reaches the person they played.

The same platforms own the relationship: the listener data, the audience, and the switch that turns an artist’s income on or off.

Why now

Phones and laptops can do the work. Matching music to a moment no longer needs a data centre; it runs on the device in your pocket.

Direct payments are now practical. Artist-direct micropayments work today, and the platform never takes custody.

Open networks matured. Durable storage and encrypted messaging exist without anyone running servers.

For grant makers

  • Artist fairnessMoney moves from listener to artist without passing through a company account.
  • Privacy by designYour library and listening history are stored on the device, not on our servers. There is no central database to sell or leak.
  • Open infrastructureBuilt on open storage and messaging networks; artists keep their work even if the apps disappear.
