Jams — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy covers the Jams apps: Jams Player, Personal Soundtrack and JamStand (together, "the Apps"). The Apps are provided by JAMS ECOSYSTEM LLC, a Michigan limited liability company ("Jams", "we", "us"). Contact: contact@jams.community.

1. How the Apps work

The Apps run on your device. We don't run servers that hold your data and we don't keep an account database. When you first open an App it creates an app identity on your device. It doesn't contain your name, but it stays the same over time, so we treat it as personal information.

2. Information the Apps handle

  • On your device only: your music library, playlists, listening history, settings, date of birth, and in Personal Soundtrack your moods, context and any wearable data you connect. We never receive these.
  • Shared with other users and artists: your app identity, any display name or avatar you set, what you play and like, items you make public, and messages, shares and chats you send. Artists receive information about plays of their tracks. Some of this sharing can't be turned off today.
  • Sent to third-party services: your IP address and the information needed for a lookup (such as a song name or your approximate location) when the Apps fetch artwork, song information, weather, podcasts or on-device models, and when you connect a wearable or other account.
  • Payments: payments are recorded on a public blockchain, where they are visible to anyone.
  • Crash reports: only if you choose to submit one.
  • Research: only if you are an adult and opt in.

3. How we use information

To run the Apps' features, connect artists with listeners, process payments you authorise, fix problems you report, and comply with law. We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or show ads.

4. Who receives information

Other users and artists; the service providers and third-party services the Apps use to work (including network, storage, music information, location, weather, payment and app-store providers), each under its own privacy policy; and others where required by law, to protect users or our rights, or in a sale of the business.

5. Public and permanent information

Some information the Apps send to other users, publish (for example, what artists publish in JamStand) or record on a blockchain is public, may be kept by others, and can't be recalled or deleted by us.

6. Retention

Information on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Information sent to others is kept by them. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of your request.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. Not all messages are encrypted, so don't share sensitive information in chats. Protect your device and back up your keys.

8. Do Not Track

The Apps don't respond to "Do Not Track" signals and contain no advertising or analytics trackers. Third-party services the Apps contact may collect information about you across sites and services under their own policies.

9. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 and block use when a birth date under 13 is entered. Some features are limited for users under 18. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising (we do neither). Most of your data is on your device, where you can delete it yourself. To make a request, email contact@jams.community.

11. Users outside the United States

The Apps are intended for users in the United States. The networks they use are global, so information may be handled in other countries.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the dates above.
